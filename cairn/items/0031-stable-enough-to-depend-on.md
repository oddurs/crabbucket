The point at which a breaking change starts costing someone other than the author something real.

Everything before this is allowed to change under anyone using it. Most of
the work here is therefore not features: it is deciding what is promised,
writing it down, and being able to keep it.

The bar is not "feature complete". It is that the API has been implemented
against twice, the design document has survived contact with the code, and
the numbers behind every claim are measured rather than assumed.
